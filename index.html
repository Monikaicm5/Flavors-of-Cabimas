Nuestra historia

A little piece of Cabimas in South Florida

Cabimas sits on the shores of Lake Maracaibo in Zulia, Venezuela: hot sun, big flavor, and food made to share. We brought that home cooking to North Lauderdale: arepas and cachapas made the way we grew up eating them, plus burgers with names anyone from back home will recognize: Los Médanos, El Lucero, Buena Vista, Ambrosio.

Come hungry. Leave full.

  • Arepa CabimeraThe house signature, fried and loaded Cabimas-style.
  • CachapasSweet corn, queso de mano, and your choice of meat.
  • 200 g burgersArtisan beef stacked with plantain, bacon, chips and more.
