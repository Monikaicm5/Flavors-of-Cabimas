Nuestra historia

A little piece of Lake Maracaibo in South Florida.

Cabimas sits on the shores of Lake Maracaibo in Zulia, Venezuela: hot sun, big flavor, and food made to share. We brought that home cooking to North Lauderdale, plus burgers with names anyone from back home will recognize: Los Médanos, El Lucero, Buena Vista, Ambrosio. Come hungry. Leave full.
